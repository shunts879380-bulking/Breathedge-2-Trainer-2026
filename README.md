## 🎮 About the Game

Breathedge 2 is a humorous, story-driven space survival adventure developed by RedRuins Softworks. It entered Steam Early Access on August 31, 2026, combining exploration, crafting, resource gathering, repairs, equipment upgrades, quests, NPC interactions, and combat in a dangerous open-space setting.

---

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Breathedge 2**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life enhancements for Breathedge 2's survival and exploration systems. It can reduce the pressure of oxygen, temperature, hunger, thirst, combat damage, equipment wear, and resource shortages while preserving the game's scavenging, crafting, repair, armor, and quest mechanics.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore the story, wrecks, stations, settlements, optional locations, crafting systems, armor, and quests with less pressure from survival resources and combat risks.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

- **Infinite Health** — Prevents the Man from losing health during exploration, environmental hazards, and combat.

- **Unlimited Resources** — Keeps gathered crafting materials available for producing tools, equipment, upgrades, and repair items.
- **Easy Crafting** — Allows crafting and equipment preparation with reduced resource limitations.

- **Damage Modifier** — Adjusts outgoing damage to make encounters with hostile enemies and other threats easier to manage.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer after entering the game world and loading a save. Unlimited oxygen is most useful during long open-space journeys, while temperature protection helps when crossing hazardous or cold areas. Keep normal movement and navigation controls unchanged so the trainer only affects survival limits.

### Tools & Inventory

Collect resources and blueprints normally, then activate unlimited resources or easy crafting when preparing tools, equipment, upgrades, and repairs. The trainer does not replace the need to discover blueprints or interact with the machines and objects required by the current Early Access systems.

### World & Environment

Explore abandoned stations, ruined settlements, shipwrecks, and other optional locations at your own pace. Survival options are useful when gathering junk, repairing machinery, dismantling equipment, solving environmental obstacles, completing side quests, and searching for collectibles far from safe areas.

### Combat & Weapons

Activate health, defense, or damage options before entering hostile areas. Armor remains part of the equipment system, with different protective items suited to cold, combat, and radiation. Disable combat assists when you want encounters to retain their intended challenge.
